ABOUT NETHAWK SOLUTIONS

Engineering Solutions for a Connected World.

TECHNOLOGY. INTELLIGENCE. CAPABILITY.

NETHAWK SOLUTIONS is a technology and solutions company delivering integrated digital, engineering, automation, security, and defence technology capabilities. We combine software engineering, intelligent systems, unmanned technologies, data, automation, and technical training to help organisations improve operational efficiency, security, situational awareness, and decision-making.

NETHAWK SOLUTIONS is building a multidisciplinary technology ecosystem designed to solve complex operational and business challenges. Our approach is built around practical technology, indigenous capability, innovation, and long-term technical support.

OUR MISSION

Intelligence-Driven Operational Effectiveness

To deliver innovative, reliable, and intelligence-driven technology solutions that improve operational effectiveness, protect critical assets, and enable organisations to work smarter and respond faster.

OUR VISION

Global Recognition & Indigenous Advancement

To become a globally recognised technology solutions company, advancing Africa's digital and engineering capabilities through innovation, excellence, and practical technology.
